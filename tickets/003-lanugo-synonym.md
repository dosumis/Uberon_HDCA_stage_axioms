# Synonym request: lanugo

**Ontology:** Uberon
**Type:** synonym addition (not a new term)

## Request

Add **lanugo** as a synonym of an existing hair class — most likely
`primary hair` (UBERON:0037461), if that is the right referent.

## Why

Lanugo is the fine unpigmented hair that covers the fetus from roughly 20 weeks
and is normally shed before or shortly after term. It is the standard term in
obstetrics, neonatology and forensic age estimation, and it is how fetal skin
samples get labelled.

Searching Uberon via OLS4 for "lanugo" returns `coat of hair` (UBERON:0010166)
and `primary hair` (UBERON:0037461), neither carrying it as a synonym. If
`primary hair` is the intended referent then this is a synonym gap rather than a
missing class, and a synonym is the cheaper fix.

If lanugo is **not** equivalent to primary hair — for instance if primary hair
is a broader developmental category — then a distinct class would be warranted
instead. I have not been able to establish which from the existing definitions,
so I am raising it as a question rather than asserting the answer.

## Searches performed

- Uberon via OLS4, "lanugo": no term carries it as a label or synonym.
- Uberon labels and exact synonyms: no hits.

## Context

Encountered while surveying Uberon's coverage of fetal-stage structures for
annotating single-cell atlases.
