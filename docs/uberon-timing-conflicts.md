# Uberon Timing Conflicts

*Uberon quality report: human developmental timing*

Six groups of Uberon axioms that cannot hold in human, given when the structures involved exist.
Each was found automatically, by combining Uberon's `develops_from`, `part_of` and subclass
axioms with Carnegie-stage bounds curated for human. Each comes with the axioms involved, the
bounds they collide with, and the derivation.

## How these were found

Each structure's existence is treated as two time points, a start and an end, and each
Carnegie stage likewise. Every axiom becomes an ordering constraint between points. A conflict
is a set of constraints that would put some point strictly before itself. The reasoning is a
transitive closure in Datalog (Soufflé), so every reported conflict comes with the exact
statements that produce it.

Three readings of Uberon relations are assumed. They are where an editor may reasonably
disagree:

- **X `develops_from` Y**: X starts to exist after Y starts to exist.
- **X `part_of` Y**: X exists only while Y does, so X starts no earlier and ends no later than
  Y. This is the "at all times the part exists" reading. Four of the six issues depend on it,
  and each says so.
- **X `SubClassOf` Y**: every axiom on Y holds of X.

Stage bounds come from the taxon-stage-axioms table. A bound marked **sourced** rests on a
verbatim passage from O'Rahilly & Müller (1987), *Developmental Stages in Human Embryos*,
machine-checked against the text. One marked **unsourced** is a curator's background-knowledge
claim not yet checked against a source. A conflict between two sourced bounds is the strongest
evidence that the Uberon axiom between them is wrong.

## Issues

### 1. Marginal layer said to develop from mantle layer

**Assessment: likely error**

**Uberon axiom**

- neural tube marginal layer `UBERON:0004062` **develops_from** neural tube mantle layer
  `UBERON:0004061` (axiom note: "forms from axons")

**Bounds it collides with**

- marginal layer starts during **CS12** (sourced)
  > "Neurofibrils begin to form for the first time in the rhombencephalic wall, and in most
  > embryos a marginal layer begins to develop." (stage 12)
- mantle layer starts during **CS13** (sourced)
  > "The remaining wall is made up of the undifferentiated ependymal zone, except for a
  > beginning mantle zone in the region that is to form the ventral horn of gray matter."
  > (stage 13)

**Derivation**

```
start(CS13) ≤ start(mantle layer)            mantle starts during CS13
           < start(marginal layer)          marginal develops_from mantle
           < end(CS12) = start(CS13)        marginal starts during CS12
⇒ start(CS13) < start(CS13)
```

**Suggested review**

The axiom note says the marginal layer "forms from axons", which is a claim about what the
layer is made of, not about what it develops from. In human the source records a marginal
layer, together with the first neurofibrils, one stage before it records a mantle zone. A
`develops_from` edge whose precursor appears later cannot be right. Consider removing it, or
replacing it with a precursor that exists first, such as the germinal neuroepithelium, from
which Uberon already derives the mantle layer.

- Depends on part_of reading: **no**
- Also reached via: hindbrain marginal layer, cerebellum marginal layer (subclasses)

### 2. Neural plate regions said to develop from neural tube regions

**Assessment: likely error**

**Uberon axioms**

- hindbrain neural plate `UBERON:0009614` **develops_from** presumptive hindbrain
  `UBERON:0007277`
- presumptive hindbrain `UBERON:0007277` **develops_from** posterior neural tube
  `UBERON:0003076` (source: XAO-abduced)
- midbrain neural plate `UBERON:0009611` **develops_from** presumptive midbrain
  `UBERON:0009616`
- presumptive midbrain `UBERON:0009616` **develops_from** midbrain neural tube
  `UBERON:0010286` (source: ZFA)
- presumptive midbrain `UBERON:0009616` **develops_from** anterior neural tube
  `UBERON:0003080`

**Bounds it collides with**

- neural tube starts during **CS10** (sourced)
- posterior neural tube starts during **CS10** (unsourced)
- neural plate starts during **CS8** (unsourced), inherited by hindbrain and midbrain neural
  plate as subclasses

**Derivation** (hindbrain; midbrain is parallel)

```
start(CS10) ≤ start(posterior neural tube)    posterior neural tube starts during CS10
           < start(presumptive hindbrain)    presumptive hindbrain develops_from posterior neural tube
           < start(hindbrain neural plate)   hindbrain neural plate develops_from presumptive hindbrain
           < end(CS08) ≤ start(CS10)        hindbrain neural plate ⊑ neural plate, starts during CS8
⇒ start(CS10) < start(CS10)
```

**Suggested review**

Read together, these axioms make a neural plate region develop from a neural tube region,
which reverses neurulation. Uberon's own structure points the same way: it also says
`hindbrain neural plate part_of chordal neural plate` and
`posterior neural tube develops_from chordal neural plate`, so the hindbrain neural plate ends
up a developmental descendant of something that develops from its own whole. The
neural-tube-to-presumptive edges carry XAO and ZFA provenance, so they may have been imported
from frog and zebrafish, where the plate-to-tube transition is organised differently.

Likely intent: the presumptive region spans plate and tube stages. Consider
`presumptive hindbrain develops_from hindbrain neural plate`, or making the plate region
`part_of` the presumptive region, in place of the current direction.

- Depends on part_of reading: **no** (the posterior neural tube variant needs only
  develops_from)

### 3. Amnion and chorion from amniotic folds, in human

**Assessment: likely error in human**

**Uberon axioms**

- amnion `UBERON:0000305` **develops_from** amniotic fold `UBERON:0005971`
  (source: ISBN:0073040584)
- chorion membrane `UBERON:0003124` **develops_from** amniotic fold `UBERON:0005971`
  (source: ISBN:0073040584)
- amniotic fold `UBERON:0005971` **develops_from** somatopleure `UBERON:0004874`
  (source: ISBN:0073040584)
- somatopleure `UBERON:0004874` **part_of** lateral plate mesoderm `UBERON:0003081`

**Bounds it collides with**

- amnion starts during **CS5** (unsourced); the amniotic cavity's CS5 start is sourced
  > "Additional features of stage 5 include the definite appearance of the amniotic cavity
  > and the formation of extra-embryonic mesoblast." (stage 5)
- lateral plate mesoderm starts during **CS8** (unsourced)
- chorionic villus (`part_of` chorion membrane) starts during **CS6** (sourced); stage 5 is
  described as "implanted but previllous"

**Derivation**

```
start(CS08) ≤ start(lateral plate mesoderm)  LPM starts during CS8
           ≤ start(somatopleure)            somatopleure part_of LPM
           < start(amniotic fold)           amniotic fold develops_from somatopleure
           < start(amnion)                  amnion develops_from amniotic fold
           < end(CS05) ≤ start(CS08)        amnion starts during CS5
⇒ start(CS08) < start(CS08)
```

**Suggested review**

The amniotic-fold route is how the amnion and chorion form in sauropsids and in some mammals.
The cited source is a comparative anatomy text (Kardong). In human the amniotic cavity, and
with it the amnion, appears by cavitation within the epiblast at stage 5, before there is any
lateral plate mesoderm or somatopleure to fold. The amniotic fold carries only an
`in_taxon Amniota` constraint, so nothing stops these axioms applying to human. Consider
taxon-scoping them, as GCIs for the taxa where folding occurs, rather than asserting them for
all amniotes.

- Depends on part_of reading: **yes** (somatopleure part_of lateral plate mesoderm)

### 4. Lung saccule said to be part of the alveolus

**Assessment: likely error**

**Uberon axiom**

- lung saccule `UBERON:0000116` **part_of** alveolus of lung `UBERON:0002299` (source: GO)

**Bounds it collides with**

- lung saccule starts during **week 24** (unsourced)
- alveolus of lung starts during **week 36** (unsourced)

**Derivation**

```
start(wk36) ≤ start(alveolus)               alveolus starts during week 36
           ≤ start(lung saccule)            saccule part_of alveolus
           < end(wk24) ≤ start(wk36)        saccule starts during week 24
⇒ start(wk36) < start(wk36)
```

**Suggested review**

Both bounds are fetal and unsourced. They come from outside the corpus, which ends at stage 23.
But the order they encode is the standard one: terminal saccules form in the saccular period,
and alveoli form later by septation of the saccules. A saccule is the precursor of alveoli, not
a part of one. Consider `alveolus of lung develops_from lung saccule`, with the saccule
`part_of` the lung or the pulmonary acinus.

- Depends on part_of reading: **yes**, though the axiom looks inverted on its own terms

### 5. Otic pit said to be part of the otic placode

**Assessment: probable error**

**Uberon axiom**

- otic pit `UBERON:0006273` **part_of** otic placode `UBERON:0003069`

**Bounds it collides with**

- otic placode ends during **CS11** (sourced); the source supports the placode's CS9 start
  directly, and its CS11 end only indirectly
- otic pit ends during **CS12** (unsourced)

**Derivation**

```
start(CS12) ≤ end(otic pit)                 pit ends during CS12
           ≤ end(otic placode)              pit part_of placode
           < end(CS11) = start(CS12)        placode ends during CS11
⇒ start(CS12) < start(CS12)
```

**Suggested review**

The otic pit is the placode after it has invaginated. It is the next stage of the same tissue,
so it outlives the placode as a named structure, and that is why it cannot be a part of it.
Consider `otic pit develops_from otic placode`, or `transformation_of`, in place of `part_of`.

- Depends on part_of reading: **yes**

### 6. Chorionic villi made to postdate the allantois

**Assessment: needs review**

**Uberon axioms**

- chorionic villus `UBERON:0007106` **develops_from** chorionic plate `UBERON:0004027`
  (source: MP-def)
- chorionic plate `UBERON:0004027` **part_of** placental disc `UBERON:8600038`
- placental disc `UBERON:8600038` **part_of** placenta `UBERON:0001987`
- placenta `UBERON:0001987` **develops_from** allantois `UBERON:0004340`
  (source: ISBN:0073040584)

**Bounds it collides with**

- chorionic villus starts during **CS6** (sourced); inherited by primary chorionic villus
  `UBERON:0009993` and the other villus subclasses
  > "Characteristic features: implanted but previllous; …" (stage 5)
- allantois starts during **CS7** (unsourced)

**Derivation**

```
start(CS07) ≤ start(allantois)              allantois starts during CS7
           < start(placenta)                placenta develops_from allantois
           ≤ start(placental disc)          placental disc part_of placenta
           ≤ start(chorionic plate)         chorionic plate part_of placental disc
           < start(chorionic villus)        chorionic villus develops_from chorionic plate
           < end(CS06) = start(CS07)        chorionic villus starts during CS6
⇒ start(CS07) < start(CS07)
```

**Suggested review**

The chain places every chorionic villus after the definitive placenta, and so after the
allantois. Primary villi, a subclass, form from trophoblast at stage 6, before the allantois
appears. Which axiom gives way is a modelling decision. One option:
`chorionic villus develops_from chorionic plate` comes from a mouse phenotype definition and may
suit the definitive villous tree but not the first villi. Another:
`placenta develops_from allantois` may overstate the allantois's role in a chorionic human
placenta, where it mainly contributes umbilical vessels. Uberon also has placenta
`develops_from` chorion membrane and uterus.

- Depends on part_of reading: **yes** (the two part_of links)
- The allantois bound is unsourced

## Also found, not Uberon issues

### HsapDv: Carnegie stage 05a contradicts itself

`HsapDv:0000031` 'Carnegie stage 05a' is asserted both `part_of` and `immediately_preceded_by`
`HsapDv:0000009` 'Carnegie stage 05'. A part cannot begin when its whole ends, so the stage
ontology is inconsistent on its own. Presumably the intended predecessor is Carnegie stage 04.
This edge was withheld from the run; it is for the HsapDv tracker.

### Generic bound inherited by a late subclass

The table's point bound "neural tube marginal layer starts during CS12" is inherited by
*cerebellum marginal layer*, which is `part_of` the cerebellum, which `develops_from` the
rhombic lip. The rhombic lip's CS16 start is sourced. The Uberon axioms are fine. The fault is
a point bound on a generic class whose subclasses start at different times. It will be fixed in
the table.

### Neural groove outlives the neural plate

`neural groove part_of neural plate` collides with the table's neural plate end at CS10
(unsourced) against a groove end at CS11. Neural tube closure runs on to CS12 caudally, so the
plate's end bound is the likelier culprit. Not raised against Uberon.
